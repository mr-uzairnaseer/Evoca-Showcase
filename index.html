Who we are.

Our Story

Evoca Enterprises Ltd was founded with a clear ambition: to create premium soft drinks without pricing them into a niche category. From the outset, we committed to producing drinks we would genuinely choose ourselves. This meant refusing to compromise on taste, ingredient quality, or production standards.

We would not produce fruit drinks without real fruit.
We would not rely on controversial colourings.
We would not use alcohol-based solvents in our formulations.

That commitment led to industry-first products and a brand built on integrity.

Built on Principle

Founded by Intisar Qasim (CEO) and Asif Salahuddin (COO), Evoca Drinks combines leadership experience with long-term commercial vision. Despite entering one of the most competitive markets in the world, Evoca has sold over 1.6 million cans with minimal marketing support — demonstrating strong consumer demand for a credible, premium alternative.
